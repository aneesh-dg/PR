WHAT:
Adding the ability for customers to provide their WhatsApp number as an alternative notification method. Those who provide this alternative number shall receive the notification via email and WhatsApp.

WHY:

Customers have requested for the provision of receiving the notification through WhatsApp as well as email, as WhatsApp is more handy.

Notes:

WhatsApp notification is optional. Customers who do not provide a WhatsApp number will continue to receive notifications through email only.
